Project #04 • v20 ADVANCED • by Aryan Kumar Shah • Online translation uses the free MyMemory API (no key, no account — it has a daily limit, so very long texts may be cut). If the internet/API fails, common English ↔ Hindi/Bengali phrases are translated offline from a built-in phrase book, and other common words are translated word-by-word offline — the app tells you which source it used. No text is uploaded to GitHub — history and favourites stay only on your phone.
